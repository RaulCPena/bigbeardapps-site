The Studio

From the Air Force to the App Store.

Big Beard Apps is a one-person iOS studio run by Raul Peña — an Air Force veteran and licensed real estate broker who taught himself to code, building small, private apps that respect the people using them.

Hi, I'm Raul.

I build Big Beard Apps out of Georgetown, Texas — a one-person iOS studio, run around a full-time engineering job and family life, on a stubborn belief that software should work for the people using it, not the other way around.

My path here wasn't a straight line, and that shaped everything: your data belongs on your device. No accounts, no ads, no trackers, nothing sold to anyone. Where an app genuinely needs a server, it sends the least it can and remembers nothing about you.
