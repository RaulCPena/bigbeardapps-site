The Studio

Built by one person, with care.

Big Beard Apps is a one-person iOS studio run by Raul Pena — building small, private apps that respect the people using them.

Hi, I'm Raul.

I build iOS apps out of Texas as a one-person studio. Big Beard Apps started from a simple frustration: too many apps treat the people using them as the product — ads, tracking, accounts you never asked for, data quietly sold on.

So I build the opposite. Every Big Beard app is built around one idea: your data belongs on your device. No accounts required, no ads, no trackers, nothing sold to third parties. Where an app needs a server, it sends the least it can and keeps nothing about you.

The apps

Feastmark turns recipe videos from Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube into your personal cookbook — even when the recipe is only spoken in the video. PayoffPilot is a private debt-payoff planner: compare Snowball and Avalanche with your real numbers, run what-ifs, and track your way to debt-free. One-time purchase, never a subscription.

Why "Big Beard"

Because I have one, and because the apps are built the way I'd want them built for myself. If you want to follow along or reach out, I'm on X, Mastodon, and Bluesky — or just use the contact form.
