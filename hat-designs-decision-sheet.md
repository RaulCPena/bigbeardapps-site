# Hat Design Decision Sheet
**Date:** September 25, 2026

## Overview
Three trucker cap designs for Big Beard Apps brands using **Flexfit 6511** style hats from TheHatPros.com

---

## 1. Big Beard Apps
**Hat Color:** Navy blue front / White mesh back  
**Logo Method:** Woven fabric patch (sewn on)  
**Logo Design:** Bearded developer with headphones, coffee cup, code brackets  
**Side Embroidery:** bigbeardapps.com (white thread)  
**Target Audience:** Developers, tech enthusiasts, beard lovers  
**Vibe:** Solo indie developer grinding away

**Colors in logo:**
- Navy blue
- Bright cyan
- Orange accent
- White

---

## 2. HuntMark  
**Hat Color:** Forest green front / Forest green mesh back  
**Logo Method:** Woven fabric patch (sewn on)  
**Logo Design:** Topographic contour lines with orange tree location marker (logo only, no text)  
**Side Embroidery:** huntmark.app (orange thread)  
**Target Audience:** Hunters, outdoorsmen, public land users  
**Vibe:** Outdoor adventure, topographic maps, hunting spots

**Colors in logo:**
- Forest green (multiple shades)
- Bright orange

---

## 3. Gunmark
**Hat Color:** Black front / Black mesh back  
**Logo Method:** Direct embroidery (metallic gold thread)  
**Logo Design:** Concentric circle target/reticle with 'G' and diamond (logo only, no text)  
**Side Embroidery:** gunmark.app (gold thread)  
**Target Audience:** Firearm collectors, gun enthusiasts  
**Vibe:** Tactical, precision, firearms documentation

**Colors in logo:**
- Gold/amber
- Black

---

## Hat Specifications
- **Model:** Flexfit 6511 Trucker Cap
- **Construction:** Cotton twill front panels, mesh back
- **Style:** 6-panel structured
- **Closure:** Plastic snapback
- **Fit:** Adjustable
- **Manufacturer:** TheHatPros.com

---

## Manufacturing Methods
1. **Woven Patches** (Big Beard Apps, HuntMark): Captures fine detail, sewn onto hat
2. **Direct Embroidery** (Gunmark): Gold metallic thread, clean geometric design
3. **Side Text**: Standard single-line embroidery on side panel
