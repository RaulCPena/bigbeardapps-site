Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

The short version: ReelTalk is an iMessage sticker pack with a small gallery screen for browsing the stickers. Everything runs on your device — it has no network access, collects nothing, and tracks nothing. There is no data to have a policy about — but Apple requires this page, so here it is.

What ReelTalk Is

ReelTalk is a sticker pack for iMessage made by Big Beard Apps ("we," "us"). The stickers live in your Messages sticker drawer, and the app includes a small gallery screen for browsing the pack. Everything runs entirely on your device — there is no server, no network access, and no code that phones home.

Information We Collect

None. ReelTalk does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, or analytics — it has no network access at all. It never sees your messages, your contacts, or which stickers you send.

Purchases

ReelTalk is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Purchases can be restored on any device signed in to your Apple Account.

Third-Party Services

None. No analytics, no advertising networks, no data processors.

Children's Privacy

ReelTalk collects no information from anyone, including children. The stickers are family-friendly fishing puns.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Given that ReelTalk has no network access, we don't expect much news here.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us via the contact form.
